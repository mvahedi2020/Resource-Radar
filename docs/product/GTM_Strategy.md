# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy: Resource Radar

## 1. Value Proposition
Stop burning out your best engineers. Resource Radar visualizes team capacity so you can plan sprints with confidence, not guesswork.

## 2. Target Market
- **Primary ICP:** High-growth tech companies (Series A to Series C) where processes are starting to break down due to scale.
- **Buyer Persona:** VP of Engineering, Director of Engineering.
- **User Persona:** Engineering Managers, Agile Coaches, TPMs.

## 3. Pricing Strategy
- **Free Tier:** Up to 10 engineers. Perfect for a single squad to adopt and prove value.
- **Pro Tier:** $15/engineer/month. Includes Jira integration and historical burnout reporting.
- **Enterprise Tier:** Custom pricing. Includes predictive capacity modeling and SLA.

## 4. Acquisition Channels
- **Product-Led Growth (PLG):** The Free Tier allows a single Engineering Manager to adopt the tool for their team. When they show the beautiful heatmap in the cross-functional leadership meeting, other EMs will want it, driving viral internal adoption.
- **Content Marketing:** Publish case studies on "The true cost of developer burnout" and "How Spotify does capacity planning."

## 5. Launch Milestones
- **Month 1:** Launch to our Beta waitlist of 50 Engineering Managers.
- **Month 3:** Public launch on Product Hunt with a "Burnout Calculator" lead magnet.
