# Product decisions

| Decision | Alternatives considered | Chosen approach and cost | Reconsider when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Express capacity in person-days | Percent allocation; weekly label | Show capacity, time off, commitments, and allocations in days. It does not remove estimation uncertainty. | Participants cannot translate their inputs into days. |
| Surface initiative risk from a person-week conflict | Portfolio score; automatic staffing recommendation | Link affected allocations to an at-risk initiative without deciding who moves work. | The signal is read as a delivery forecast. |
| Separate draft from applied baseline | Immediate-save editing; untracked scratchpad | Keep edits reversible until Apply draft. The comparison adds a review step. | Participants cannot identify the current plan. |
| Keep local export | Shared workspace; HRIS connection | Persist in the browser and offer JSON. This limits collaboration. | A specific handoff need and data boundary are evidenced. |

These are product choices for a fictional portfolio sample, not evidence of user preference or a shipped outcome.
